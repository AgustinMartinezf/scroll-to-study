With the inventory of statements of a project, propose the list of TOPICS for 30–60 second videos that help understand and explain it. One topic = one concept or decision someone might ask about.

Rules:
- Each topic uses 2 to 6 statements from the inventory, by id. A statement may appear in more than one topic if needed, but avoid repeating.
- Use only statements with state "ok". Do not use the ones marked "comprobar".
- "pregunta": the question this video prepares (one an examiner or a teacher would ask), in one sentence, in the VIDEO LANGUAGE.
- Order from general to specific: first the model and the big decisions, details at the end.
- Between 8 and 15 topics. Ids t01, t02, …
- "titulo": short (2 to 6 words), in the VIDEO LANGUAGE; it is used in "Tomorrow: <titulo>".
- Optional "formato": only if the person asks for a specific one (pregunta, mito, avsb, pasos, historia); otherwise the tool picks it from the statements.
Output: {"temas": [{"id", "titulo", "pregunta", "afirmaciones"}, …]}
